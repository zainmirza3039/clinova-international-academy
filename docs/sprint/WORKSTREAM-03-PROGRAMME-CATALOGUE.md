# Workstream 03 — Programme catalogue

## Objective
Deliver a coherent public catalogue that lets clinicians and healthcare professionals discover relevant CPD and professional education programmes.

## Ownership
- Programme data contract and editorial status
- Catalogue browse/search/filter/sort
- Programme detail pages and clear enquiry/application calls to action
- Accurate delivery mode, audience, learning outcomes, prerequisites, duration and status
- Draft/published visibility rules

## Acceptance criteria
- Only editorially approved and genuinely available programmes appear as published.
- Programme cards and detail pages use one consistent source of truth.
- Empty, loading and error states are handled.
- Filters and links work on mobile and desktop.
- No programme is represented as accredited unless documentary evidence supports that exact claim.
- CPD is not misrepresented as self-study where the programme is instructor-led or otherwise facilitated.

## Exclusions
No learner authentication, payment, assessment, certificate issuance or admin role management.

## Dependencies
Public-site navigation from Workstream 02; enrolment handoff contract from Workstream 05.

## Verification
Catalogue discovery tests, detail-route tests, draft visibility tests, responsive and accessibility checks.
